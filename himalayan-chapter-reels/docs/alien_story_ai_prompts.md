# "Signal from Tapovan": prompt pack for a realistic AI-video version

Use these in Gemini / Veo, Kling, Runway or Sora. Generate each shot as a **9:16 vertical, ~8-second clip**.
Send the clips back and they get the same edit as the animated version: screen text, speech bubbles,
name tags, date/time, booking card, music and sound effects.

**Rules for every shot** (paste at the end of each prompt):
> Photorealistic cinematic VFX, shot on ARRI Alexa with anamorphic lenses, shallow depth of field, volumetric haze,
> moonlight and neon rim light, 9:16 vertical. **No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no captions.**

AI video tools garble text and logos, so we add the real logo, names and dates in the edit.

**For consistent characters:** first generate one still image of each alien (and the UFO) in Gemini or another image tool,
then use image-to-video / "ingredients" with that still for every shot they appear in.

## Character bible (keep this wording identical in every prompt)

- **YAKSH (alien 1):** a slender humanoid alien with smooth teal-green iridescent skin, faint bioluminescent freckles,
  large glossy black almond-shaped eyes, two thin antennae with softly glowing lime-green tips, wearing a black oversized
  hoodie with cyan drawstrings and over-ear DJ headphones with glowing cyan LED rings. Calm, cool, friendly expression.
- **BHAV (alien 2):** a stocky humanoid alien with violet skin, three glowing amber eyes (the middle one larger), a wide
  playful grin, a dark knit beanie, a purple bomber jacket and DJ headphones with glowing pink LED rings. Energetic.
- **The ship:** a classic polished chrome flying saucer with a glass dome, a ring of small multicolour lights around the
  rim and a teal underglow.
- **The venue:** an intimate rooftop café in Tapovan, Rishikesh at night under a huge full moon: warm string lights, a
  wooden DJ booth, young travellers and locals, dark Himalayan foothills and the lights of the Ganga valley below.

## Shots

1. **Cockpit (0–8s).** Inside the saucer's cockpit, close-up of YAKSH and BHAV side by side at a glowing control console;
   through the curved window behind them a full moon and a purple-teal nebula. A holographic waveform pulses on the glass.
   YAKSH tilts his head, listening; BHAV turns to him in surprise. Slow push-in.
2. **Past the moon (8–12s).** Wide space shot: the saucer glides past an enormous full moon, lights blinking, then banks
   toward Earth. Slow tracking shot, stars, lens flare.
3. **Hyperspace (12–14s).** The saucer accelerates; stars stretch into light streaks; flash of white light.
4. **Arrival over the Himalayas (14–22s).** Night aerial over snow-capped Himalayan peaks and pine forest under a full
   moon; the saucer descends toward a rooftop café with string lights in Tapovan; people on the rooftop look up and point.
5. **Landing (22–28s).** The saucer hovers above the rooftop; a teal tractor beam shines down onto the DJ booth;
   YAKSH and BHAV materialise in the beam behind the decks, put on their headphones. Crowd silhouettes in the foreground.
6. **The drop (28–34s).** Wide: YAKSH and BHAV DJing on the rooftop booth, violet/cyan/green lasers sweeping over the
   mountains, haze, the crowd jumping with hands up, the saucer hovering above with pulsing lights, full moon behind.
7. **YAKSH close-up (34–37s).** Low-angle close-up of YAKSH scratching a vinyl record, antennae glowing, lasers behind.
8. **BHAV close-up (37–40s).** Close-up of BHAV throwing both hands up, three eyes glowing, crowd cheering, confetti in the lasers.
9. **Moon (40–44s).** The full moon over the Himalayas pulsing with light; lasers crossing in front of it.

Shots 44–60s (date/time and the "THE ALIENS HAVE RSVP'D. HAVE YOU?" booking card) are added in the edit.
