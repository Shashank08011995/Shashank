FAQ

Good questions

Do I need a driving licence?

You need a valid two-wheeler licence to ride. If you don't have one, you can sit pillion with one of our riders.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, shoes you can walk in, sunscreen, and swimwear if you want to get in the water. We bring everything else.

Is it safe?

Helmets are mandatory, our guides know every road, and we ride at an easy pace with regular stops.

Can I book for just myself?

Yes. Solo travellers join a small group for the day.

What if it rains?

We adjust the route. If the weather makes the day unsafe, we move you to another date or refund you.

Where does the day start?

We pick you up from your stay anywhere in Rishikesh, Tapovan or Laxman Jhula.
